DeenBit v2.0

Our vision for a custom educational computer designed in South Africa — inspired by the micro:bit, shaped around the crescent, and built for learning, creativity and experimentation.

From micro:bit to DeenBit

DeenBit currently uses the BBC micro:bit as a powerful platform for introducing programming, electronics and sensors.

DeenBit v2.0 explores the next step: designing our own board while keeping the simplicity and hands-on learning experience that makes the micro:bit so accessible.

A board with its own identity

A custom educational board shaped around the crescent, with a focus on local design, learning and experimentation.

Designed here. Built here. Learned on everywhere.

The long-term DeenBit vision is bigger than a single device. We want to explore how locally designed educational technology can help develop programming, electronics, engineering and manufacturing skills in South Africa.

DeenBit v2.0 is currently a concept and development journey. The final hardware, components and design may evolve as we prototype and learn.
