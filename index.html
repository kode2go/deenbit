Small device. Lots to explore.

DeenBit comes with fun built-in features, but the best part is that you can program it yourself and make it your own.

📿

Button A — Thikr Counter

Count your thikr and learn how buttons, variables and displays work.

🌡️

Button B — Temperature

Use the built-in sensor to explore temperature and simple sensor programming.

🧭

A + B — Qibla Compass

Explore direction, compass sensing and how technology can help find Qibla.

👏

Clap — Night Light

Use sound detection to switch a light on and off with a clap.

💡

LED Display

Create icons, animations, messages and your own custom displays.

💻

Program It Yourself

Change the code, add new ideas and turn DeenBit into your own project.

Learn. Build. Experiment.

DeenBit is made for anyone curious about technology. Start with the built-in features, then connect it to a computer and experiment with your own programs. No experience needed — just curiosity.
