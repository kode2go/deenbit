Help build the next generation: DeenBit v2.0

What if we could design and build our own educational electronics locally? DeenBit v2.0 is a vision for learning kits inspired by the accessibility and creativity of platforms like the micro:bit, shaped by our communities and their needs.

Through initiatives like Jazari Institute, we want to bring together makers, educators, engineers, students and supporters to explore locally designed hardware, open learning resources, interactive learning tools and practical projects.
