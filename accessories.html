DeenBit Accessories

Add lights, sensors, motors and other electronics to your DeenBit. Each accessory is another chance to learn how real electronics and programming work.

Ideas to expand your DeenBit

Start simple, then connect new components and create your own projects.

💡

Night Light Kit

Add external LEDs and build your own clap-controlled bedside light, Ramadan lantern or decorative project.

Level: Beginner
Learn: LEDs · GPIO · sound sensing
🔌

GPIO & Crocodile Clip Kit

Connect your DeenBit to LEDs, buzzers, switches and other simple components without needing to solder.

Level: Beginner
Learn: Inputs · outputs · circuits
🔊

Sound Kit

Experiment with buzzers and sound output. Create alarms, notifications, games and your own audio feedback.

Level: Beginner
Learn: Sound · timing · output
🌱

Plant Sensor Kit

Measure soil moisture and build a simple plant reminder that tells you when your plant may need water.

Level: Intermediate
Learn: Sensors · data · conditions
🚦

Traffic Light Kit

Program red, amber and green LEDs to learn sequencing, timing and simple automation.

Level: Beginner
Learn: LEDs · loops · timing
⚙️

Servo Motor Kit

Make things move. Build a pointer, small gate, indicator or your own mechanical project.

Level: Intermediate
Learn: Motors · control · movement
🌙

Ramadan Project Kit

Use LEDs, sensors and your own code to create a Ramadan lantern, countdown display or family STEM project.

Level: Beginner–Intermediate
Learn: Creativity · LEDs · programming
🧰

Electronics Starter Pack

A mix of beginner components for experimenting with your DeenBit and inventing projects of your own.

Level: All levels
Learn: Electronics · prototyping · creativity
🛠️

Build Your Own

DeenBit is programmable, so you are not limited to official accessories. Connect compatible components and make something completely new.

Level: Your choice
Learn: Design · coding · experimentation
